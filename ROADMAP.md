# Roadmap

- SPI initialization, 512-byte reads/writes, CRC, capacity and timeout handling.
- Optional fat.BlockDevice integration, protocol regressions and freestanding builds.
- Validate against Benchtop's future SPI SD-card part and disk-image backend.
- Physical SPI card/board validation.
- Multi-block transfers and native STM32 SDMMC backend, retaining SPI support.
